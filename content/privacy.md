This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites. We count visits with our own analytics, which does not
identify you. Our servers keep no request logs.

## Visits to this site

When you load a page, our server records:

- the page address and the time, without any query string except campaign tags
- the website that sent you to the page (the referrer), shortened to its domain name
- campaign tags in the link (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`), if there are any
- your country, looked up from your IP address in a database on our own servers
- your type of browser, operating system and device (desktop, mobile or tablet), read from what your browser sends
  with every request

To count unique visitors without recognising you, we combine your IP address, your browser details and the site's
name with a random value that we replace every day and then delete. We keep only a one-way hash of the result. Once
the day's value is deleted, the hash can no longer be linked to you, and your visits on different days or to
different websites cannot be linked to each other.

We do not store your IP address, we set no cookies, and we store nothing on your device for this. The data does not
go to any other company. We keep the visit records for 7 days, then only daily totals (pages, referrers, campaigns,
countries, device types), which contain no personal data.

Why: to see how people find this site and which pages they read, so we can improve it. Legal basis: our legitimate
interest in understanding how the site is used (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Your IP address is used only for a moment, to
look up your country and make the hash. Because the visit records cannot be linked to you, we cannot find, show or
delete your visits in them (GDPR Article 11).

## Where it runs

This site and its visit statistics run on our own servers at Hetzner in Germany. The vulnerability snapshot that
`shipward` downloads (`data.shipward.dev`) is stored in Cloudflare R2 in the EU and delivered through Cloudflare's
network, which sees your address in order to deliver the files.

`shipward` itself sends nothing about your software: see [Security and privacy](/docs/security/).
